 HR Attrition Analysis:

This project analyzes the IBM HR Analytics dataset of 1,470 employees to understand why employees leave the company. I used SQL, Python, and Power BI to clean the data, analyze trends, and build an interactive dashboard.
Tools Used:

SQL – Wrote business queries to analyze attrition.

Python (Pandas & Matplotlib) – Cleaned the data and created charts.

Power BI – Built an interactive dashboard.

Dashboard Highlights:

Total Employees: 1,470

Employees Left: 237

Attrition Rate: 16.1%

Average Monthly Income: $6,503

The dashboard also includes filters for Department, Gender, and Job Level.

What I Analyzed:

Attrition rate across the company

Attrition by department

Attrition by job role

Attrition by age group

Overtime vs attrition

Salary comparison between employees who stayed and left

Key Insights:

Sales has the highest attrition rate.

Employees aged 18–30 leave the most.

Employees working overtime are much more likely to leave.

Employees who left earn less on average than those who stayed.
